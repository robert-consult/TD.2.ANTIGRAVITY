4.4 Sequencing + replay (required for stability at scale)

seq is monotonically increasing per ingestor stream.

api-ws pods may optionally provide best-effort replay of last N deltas if they maintain a small in-memory ring buffer per pod. (If not, client falls back to snapshot.)

Client reconnect flow: re-auth → request snapshot → apply deltas only if seq continuity holds.

This is how you prevent reconnect storms and keep mobile stable.
