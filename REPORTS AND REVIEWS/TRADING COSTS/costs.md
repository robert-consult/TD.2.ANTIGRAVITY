## Cost Model Keys

| Key | Formula |
|-----|---------|
| **EQ** | `min(max($0.005 × shares, $1), 1% × trade)` |
| **FX** | `max(1.00bp × trade, $10)` |
| **CRYPTO** | `0.18% × trade` (min $1.75, cap 1%) |
| **BOND** | first $10k @ 0.1%, remainder @ 0.025% |
| **MF** | `min(3% × trade, $14.95)` |
| **METAL** | Legacy alias only → normalized to `commodities` (EQ model) |
