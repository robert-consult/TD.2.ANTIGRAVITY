## 4) CORE GRANT: RIGHT OF FIRST OPPORTUNITY + RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL + PLACEMENT AGENCY

This Section is the commercial core of the Agreement.

### 4.1 Right of First Opportunity (ROFO) — Employment / Capital / Placement

If you become a **Selected Trader**, then before you accept any Opportunity in the Trading and Fund Management Industry, you grant Tradequip the **first opportunity** to do one or more of the following (at Tradequip’s election):

* (a) offer you employment with a Tradequip Affiliate or aligned management company;
* (b) offer you a role managing capital under Tradequip-aligned structures;
* (c) place you with a Partner Management Company; and/or
* (d) negotiate a structured pathway (interviews, trials, team assignment, compensation terms).

### 4.2 Right of First Refusal (ROFR) — Matching Third-Party Opportunities

If you receive an Opportunity (including from third parties) and you are a Selected Trader, you must first present it to Tradequip via **Notice**. Tradequip has the right to match or improve the Opportunity (including through a Partner Management Company) subject to Section 5.

### 4.3 Appointment as Placement Agent (Exclusive During “Representation Window”)

If you become a Selected Trader, you appoint Tradequip as your **exclusive representative/placement agent** for Opportunities in the Trading and Fund Management Industry during the **Representation Window**:

* **Representation Window:** begins on the date you are notified you are a Selected Trader, and lasts **6 months** (the “Initial Window”).
* **Later Moves:** If after being employed/placed through Tradequip or a Partner Management Company you later seek to change firms **within the Trading and Fund Management Industry**, you grant Tradequip an additional **6-month exclusive placement window** commencing upon your written notice that you intend to move (the “Subsequent Window”), unless an Addendum limits or converts exclusivity to non-exclusive representation.

This “agency” concept is used because, in many jurisdictions, broad post-termination non-competes are restricted, and enforceability improves when structured as **representation + non-circumvention + confidentiality** rather than pure restraint. ([GoGlobal][1])

### 4.4 Platform-Sourced Opportunities — Perpetual Priority

For **Platform-Sourced Opportunities**, you grant Tradequip a **perpetual** right to be the presenting/placing party and to receive the benefits of this Agreement (including non-circumvention and remedies), because those Opportunities arise from Tradequip’s infrastructure and network investment.

**If local law does not permit “perpetual” terms**, the relevant Addendum automatically reduces this to the **maximum enforceable period** without invalidating the remainder (see Severability/Reformation).

## 5) NOTICE + 21-DAY RESPONSE MECHANISM (ANTI-LOOPHOLE DESIGN)

### 5.1 Mandatory Notice Before Any Acceptance or Negotiation

If you are a Selected Trader and you receive any Opportunity (whether solicited or unsolicited), you must:

* (a) send Tradequip a **complete Notice** within **3 calendar days** of receipt, and
* (b) refrain from negotiating substantive terms (comp, start date, capital terms, carry, book, seeding, etc.) other than acknowledging receipt and stating: **“Please coordinate through my representative, Tradequip.”**

### 5.2 Tradequip Response Period — 21 Days (Calendar Days)

Upon Tradequip’s confirmed receipt of a **complete Notice**, Tradequip has **21 calendar days** to issue one of the following elections in writing:

* **Match/Engage:** Tradequip elects to match the Opportunity or engage you through Tradequip/Partner Management Company;
* **Waive:** Tradequip waives in writing for that specific Opportunity;
* **Request Info:** Tradequip requests missing information (tolls the clock);
* **Decline:** Tradequip declines (Trader may proceed subject to Section 5.4).

### 5.3 Tolling to Prevent “Starve-the-clock” Loopholes

The 21-day response clock **does not start** until Notice is **complete**. If Tradequip reasonably requests missing information, the clock is **paused** until you provide it.

### 5.4 Final Notice + Cooling-Off (Prevents “Immediate Alternate Employment”)

If Tradequip does not respond within the 21-day period, you must send a **Final Notice** (email + in-app message) stating Tradequip is in non-response status. After Final Notice, you must wait an additional **7 calendar days** before accepting the Opportunity.

This creates a minimum **28-day runway**, eliminating “instant bypass” behavior while preserving fairness.

### 5.5 No Acceptance / No Start / No Side Letters During the Window

Until the earlier of (i) Tradequip waiver/decline, or (ii) expiry of the 21+7 day process, you must not:

* accept the Opportunity,
* sign any agreement (including “non-binding” LOIs if they contain exclusivity),
* begin services, shadow-trade, access systems, or receive onboarding credentials,
* route the Opportunity through an affiliate to disguise it.

Any such act is **Restricted Bypass Conduct** and a material breach.

## 6) POST-SELECTION “CAPITAL / FUNDRAISE LOCKOUT” (MODULAR, WITH LOCAL FALLBACKS)

Because enforceability varies globally, this clause is **modular** and may be modified by Addendum.

### 6.1 Trigger: “Protected Benefits”

This Section applies only if you receive any of the following from Tradequip/Affiliates/Partners (“Protected Benefits”):

* (a) access to funded capital programs (real capital allocation, not pure simulation),
* (b) stipends, guaranteed payments, training stipends, travel support,
* (c) proprietary playbooks, systems, datasets, execution tooling, or confidential selection metrics,
* (d) introductions to capital providers, allocators, or seed programs arranged by Tradequip.

### 6.2 Lockout Covenant (Default Global Form)

For **[LOCKOUT_TERM_DEFAULT = 24 months]** after the later of (i) receipt of Protected Benefits, or (ii) last day you had access to Protected Benefits, you agree you will not, without Tradequip’s written consent:

* (a) solicit or raise third-party capital for a trading/fund product,
* (b) form or operate a fund/managed account program, or
* (c) manage discretionary third-party capital,
  **except** through Tradequip or Tradequip-approved counterparties.

### 6.3 Mandatory Local Modifications

If the applicable Addendum (or local law) restricts or voids such lockouts (e.g., as a restraint of trade), then **instead of a prohibition**, the lockout is automatically converted to:

* (i) a strict **non-circumvention** obligation (no Platform-Sourced counterparties),
* (ii) reinforced **confidentiality + trade secret** protections, and
* (iii) a **reasonable compensation / liquidated damages** structure tied to demonstrable platform-sourced value (where allowed), without invalidating the rest of the Agreement.

Examples of jurisdictions where post-termination restraint can be heavily limited include India (Section 27 doctrine in employment contexts), and parts of Latin America/Europe where compensation/proportionality is expected for enforceability. ([Desai & Diwanji][2])

# SCHEDULE F — REMEDIES (DEFAULT; MAY BE MODIFIED BY ADDENDUM)

**Default (Global):** If you breach Sections 4–7 by circumvention or acceptance without completion of the Notice/ROFR process, Tradequip may seek (where lawful):

* (a) injunctive relief,
* (b) actual damages, and
* (c) liquidated damages equal to the lesser of:
  i) **12 months of your total cash compensation** from the circumvented Opportunity, or
  ii) **a 20% placement-equivalent fee** on first-year compensation (or, for capital programs, **a 2% equivalent on allocated AUM for 12 months**),
  plus reasonable enforcement costs.

Where liquidated damages are treated as penalties, the Addendum converts this to **actual damages** plus lawful equitable remedies.
