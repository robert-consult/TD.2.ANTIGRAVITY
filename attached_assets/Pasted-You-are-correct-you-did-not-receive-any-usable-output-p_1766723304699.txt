## 1.2 Detection Families

### A) Multi-account on same device

**Goal:** detect two+ user accounts that share same `deviceId`.

**Rule:**

* If a `deviceId` is linked to more than 1 distinct `userId` in last N days (e.g., 30 days), flag all involved users.

**Score:**

* Base + incremental:
  `score = 35 + 10*(numOtherAccounts)` capped at 70

**Signal type:** `MULTI_ACCOUNT_DEVICE`

---

### B) IP/UA/Device churn (account sharing / remote trading)

**Goal:** detect a single account whose environment changes too frequently.

**Rules (rolling windows):**

* **IP churn**: `uniqueIPs(last 24h) >= 4`
* **UA churn**: `uniqueUserAgents(last 24h) >= 3`
* **Device churn**: `uniqueDevices(last 7d) >= 3`

**Scoring:**

* IP churn: 10 + 7*(ips-3), max 35
* UA churn: 10 + 10*(agents-2), max 30
* Device churn: 10 + 10*(devices-2), max 30

**Signal types:** `IP_CHURN`, `UA_CHURN`, `DEVICE_CHURN`

OWASP supports triggering reauthentication / controls when unusual patterns occur.
(OWASP Authentication Cheat Sheet)

---

### C) Geo “Impossible Travel” / velocity

**Goal:** detect physically impossible travel between consecutive successful logins.

**Rule:**

* Compute distance between last login geo and current login geo.
* Compute speed `km/h = distanceKm / deltaHours`
* Flag if:

  * `km/h >= 900` AND `distanceKm >= 800` AND `deltaTime <= 6h`

This is a standard “Impossible Travel” technique widely used in IAM platforms.
(Ping Identity overview)
(IPinfo steps for detection)
(Microsoft Defender notes ML/false positives handling)

**Signal type:** `GEO_VELOCITY`

---

### D) Hedging across accounts (same device/network)

**Goal:** detect coordinated buy/sell opposite direction across accounts on same symbol.

**Rule:**
When account A places trade on symbol S, side BUY:

* Look for trades opened within last 10 minutes:

  * Same symbol S
  * Opposite side SELL
  * Different userId
  * Linked by:

    * same `deviceId`, OR
    * same `ip` (optionally /24 subnet enhancement later)

If found, flag both accounts.

**Score:** fixed high severity: 55+
**Signal type:** `HEDGE_PAIR`

## 1.3 Aggregated Risk Score (per user)

You maintain a per-user rolling score:

`riskScore = clamp(sum(signalPoints), 0..100)`

Risk tiers:

* 0–39: LOW
* 40–59: MEDIUM
* 60–79: HIGH
* 80–100: CRITICAL

Store:

* score
