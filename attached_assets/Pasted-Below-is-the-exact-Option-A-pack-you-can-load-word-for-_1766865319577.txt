(a) any TradeQuip Group offer made to you; and

(a) solicit or raise third-party capital for trading/investment products;
(b) form or operate a fund/vehicle/managed account program; or
(c) accept any discretionary management of third-party capital,
and you must allow TradeQuip the ROFR/ROFO opportunity to employ/place/endorse you or decline in writing.

These Opportunity Rights are intended as commercial opportunity rights, notice, and anti-circumvention protections. 9.1 When Notice Is Required.
If you are in a Candidate Window or Selected status, or if the matter is a Platform-Linked Opportunity, you must provide Notice before you:
(a) accept, sign, commence, or begin onboarding for any Industry Opportunity;
(b) negotiate substantive terms (comp, AUM, start date, duties, exclusivity, revenue split, carry);
(c) announce fundraising or commence capital solicitation; or
(d) commence trading or managing capital for a third party.

9.2 Notice Must Be Complete.
A Notice is “complete” only if it includes all items in Schedule N (Notice Form). The response clock does not start until Notice is complete.

9.3 Standstill During Response Period.
From delivery of a complete Notice until the earlier of (i) TradeQuip’s written response, or (ii) expiration of the response process, you must not:
• accept/sign the Opportunity,
• commence work/trading/capital management,
• receive credentials, system access, or onboarding materials beyond scheduling,
• enter side letters, “trial” arrangements, shadow roles, or indirect roles,
• route through an affiliate/entity/intermediary.

9.4 TradeQuip Response Period: 21 Calendar Days.
TradeQuip has twenty-one (21) calendar days after confirmed receipt of a complete Notice to:
(a) match or present a substantially equivalent alternative (employment/capital/placement),
(b) approve the third-party Opportunity in writing,
(c) decline in writing, or
(d) request missing information (which tolls the response clock until provided).

9.5 Final Notice and Cooling-Off (No “Silent Expiry” Loophole).
If TradeQuip does not respond within the 21-day period, you must send a “Final Notice” (email + in-app submission) stating the response period has elapsed.
You must then wait an additional seven (7) calendar days before accepting or commencing the Opportunity.
This Final Notice and cooling-off requirement is an essential anti-circumvention control.

If you proceed after approval/decline, you may proceed only on the terms disclosed in your Notice. Materially improved terms require a new Notice.

10.1 No Circumvention.
You must not engage in Restricted Bypass Conduct or otherwise evade TradeQuip’s Opportunity Rights.

10.2 Platform-Linked Counterparties.
If you engage a Platform-Linked counterparty outside the Notice/ROFR process, it is presumed circumvention unless you prove otherwise.

10.3 Non-Interference.
For twelve (12) months after your last Candidate/Selected status event, you will not intentionally interfere with TradeQuip’s Partner Firm relationships connected to your Platform pathway.

11) FEES / LIQUIDATED DAMAGES / REMEDIES (UPDATED)
11.1 Employment/Engagement Circumvention Fee.
If you breach Sections 8–10 and accept/commence an Industry Opportunity (employment/engagement) without full compliance, you agree to pay TradeQuip a placement-equivalent fee equal to:
FORTY PERCENT (40%) of your first-year total cash compensation.
“First-year total cash compensation” means base salary + guaranteed bonus + sign-on bonus + guaranteed allowances paid or guaranteed within the first 12 months of start.

11.2 Capital Program / AUM Circumvention Fee.
If you breach Sections 8–10 and commence discretionary management of allocated third-party capital without compliance, you agree to pay:
FOUR PERCENT (4%) of the allocated AUM for twelve (12) months,
payable monthly as 1/12 of 4% based on gross AUM allocated to you or to a vehicle you manage/control.

TradeQuip may seek payment from you and, where lawful, from the counterparty and/or any entity you control that receives the economics.

11.4 Not a Penalty; Court Reduction.
The parties agree damages are difficult to estimate and these amounts are a reasonable pre-estimate of loss.
If a court finds any amount is an unenforceable penalty, the amount automatically reduces to the maximum enforceable amount and the remainder of the Agreement remains effective.

11.5 Injunctive Relief / Specific Performance.
Breach may cause irreparable harm. TradeQuip may seek injunctive relief where available, in addition to monetary remedies.

12) DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY (GLOBAL BASELINE)

TradeQuip processes personal data and performance data for account creation, platform delivery, security, fraud prevention, evaluation, ranking, selection, compliance, partner placement, dispute handling, and improvement.

12.2 Cross-Border Transfers.

12.3 Addendum Controls.

14) SEVERABILITY / REFORMATION / MAXIMUM ENFORCEABLE CONSTRUCTION
14.1 Severability.
If any provision is invalid, the remainder remains enforceable.

14.2 Reformation.
Where permitted, any overbroad restriction will be narrowed to the minimum scope necessary to be enforceable.
