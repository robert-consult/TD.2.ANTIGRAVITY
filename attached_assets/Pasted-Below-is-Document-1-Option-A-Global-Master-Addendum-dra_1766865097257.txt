## 6) OPPORTUNITY RIGHTS (THE CORE ECONOMIC/CONTROL TERMS)

These provisions are designed to remain enforceable even where “non-compete” clauses are restricted by using (i) **right of first opportunity/refusal**, (ii) **notice**, (iii) **non-circumvention**, and (iv) **liquidated damages/placement-equivalent fees**.

### 6.1 Status Levels That Trigger Obligations

Your obligations depend on your status:

**(A) General Member (default):** You have confidentiality and anti-fraud obligations; no exclusivity yet.

**(B) Opportunity Candidate:** TradeQuip notifies you in writing that you are eligible for introductions, interviews, placement, or capital programs.

**(C) Selected Group:** TradeQuip notifies you in writing that you are shortlisted/selected for hire, placement, or capital allocation.

### 6.2 Right of First Opportunity (ROFO) — Opportunity Candidate

When you become an **Opportunity Candidate**, you grant TradeQuip a **Right of First Opportunity** for **6 months** (the “Candidate Exclusivity Window”):

* TradeQuip has the first right to present you to Partner Firms, negotiate roles, and propose TradeQuip Group employment/capital allocation.
* During this 6-month window, you must route **Industry Opportunities** (defined in Schedule A) through TradeQuip first (Section 7 notice applies).
* If a jurisdiction requires licensing for placement/agency, this clause operates only to the extent legally permitted, and TradeQuip may perform through licensed partners (Section 12.7).

### 6.3 Right of First Refusal (ROFR) — Selected Group (and Platform-Linked Opportunities)

When you enter the **Selected Group**, you grant TradeQuip a **Right of First Refusal** on:

* any TradeQuip Group offer; and
* any **Platform-Linked Opportunity** (any Opportunity connected to Platform participation as defined).

**ROFR Mechanics:**

* You must provide a Notice of the third-party offer (Section 7).
* TradeQuip has **21 calendar days** to match or present a substantially equivalent offer (compensation or AUM terms, role scope, and reasonable commercial terms).
* You may not accept, sign, commence work, commence managing capital, or fundraise under that offer until the Notice Period expires or TradeQuip responds in writing earlier.

### 6.4 Limited Post-Selection Industry Lockout (Time-Limited; Addendum Can Modify)

**Default global position (subject to Addendum):** For **12 months** after the later of:
(i) your entry into the Selected Group, or
(ii) the end of any TradeQuip Group employment/capital program discussions that you initiated or received via the Platform,
you agree not to **circumvent** TradeQuip regarding **Industry Opportunities**, meaning:

* you will not accept or commence an Industry Opportunity **without providing Notice** and honoring the ROFR/ROFO process; and
* if you do accept such an opportunity without compliance, you will owe the fees in Section 9.

This is **not drafted as a blanket “ban from working”**; it is drafted as a **notice + ROFR + anti-circumvention + fee** framework to maximize enforceability.

### 6.5 Fund Formation / Fundraising / Capital Management Controls (Structured as ROFR + Anti-Circumvention)

If you are in the Selected Group (or if the contemplated activity is Platform-Linked), then for the **Capital Control Period** (default **3 years**, Addendum may modify), you agree:

* You will not **fundraise**, **solicit capital**, **market a fund**, **form a fund/vehicle**, or **manage third-party capital** in the Trading & Capital Management Industry **in a manner that circumvents TradeQuip**.
* You must provide Notice and allow TradeQuip to:
  (a) employ you via the TradeQuip Group; or
  (b) place you with a Partner Firm; or
  (c) endorse a firm/program in writing; or
  (d) decline in writing.

If you proceed without complying, you owe the fees in Section 9.

## 7) NOTICE PROCESS (21 DAYS) + “NO IMMEDIATE SWITCH” SEAL

### 7.1 When Notice is required

You must give Notice if, during a Restricted Period, you:
(a) receive an Industry Opportunity;
(b) are invited to interview or negotiate for an Industry Opportunity;
(c) intend to start a fund/fundraise/manage third-party capital; or
(d) intend to join a firm in the Trading & Capital Management Industry.

### 7.2 Notice content

Notice must include:

* the legal name of the firm/investor/counterparty;
* role description;
* compensation and/or AUM terms;
* start date;
* location/country; and
* all written materials offered (redactions for unrelated personal info allowed).

### 7.3 The Notice Period and standstill

TradeQuip has **21 calendar days** from confirmed receipt to respond. During that period you must not:

* sign/accept the offer;
* commence work;
* commence trading or managing capital for that firm;
* announce fundraising;
* use an intermediary to “start now and paper later.”

Any attempt to start informally, through an affiliate, through a friend’s vehicle, through a “consulting” wrapper, or through an offshore entity you control is treated as circumvention.

### 7.4 TradeQuip response outcomes

TradeQuip may:

* match the offer (employment or capital);
* propose a substantially equivalent alternative;
* approve the third-party opportunity; or
* decline.

If TradeQuip declines in writing, you may proceed without fee **only if** you do so on the terms disclosed in your Notice.

## 8) NON-SOLICITATION + NON-INTERFERENCE (ENFORCEMENT-FRIENDLY CORE)

For **12 months** after your last Platform-Linked Opportunity event (or longer if Addendum allows), you will not:

* solicit TradeQuip employees/contractors to leave;
* solicit Partner Firms introduced by TradeQuip to bypass TradeQuip;
* interfere with TradeQuip’s relationships tied to your Platform pathway.

## 9) FEES, LIQUIDATED DAMAGES, AND PAYMENT STRUCTURE (UPDATED)

This Section is intended to fairly compensate TradeQuip for discovery/placement value and deter circumvention.

### 9.1 Placement-Equivalent Fee (Employment)

If you breach Sections 6–8 and accept/commence an Industry Opportunity (employment/engagement) without complying, you agree to pay TradeQuip a fee equal to:

* **40% of your first-year total cash compensation**, defined as base salary + guaranteed bonus + sign-on bonus + guaranteed allowances tied to employment, earned or guaranteed within 12 months of start.

### 9.2 Capital Program Equivalent Fee (AUM)

If you breach Sections 6–8 and commence a capital allocation / funded trading / external AUM management arrangement without complying, you agree to pay:

* **4% of allocated AUM for 12 months**, calculated on gross AUM allocated to you (or to a vehicle you manage/control) and payable monthly as **1/12 of 4%**.

### 9.3 Who pays (Trader and/or Firm)

You agree TradeQuip may pursue payment from:
(a) you, and/or (b) the hiring firm/counterparty, if legally permitted, and/or (c) any entity you control that receives the economics.

### 9.4 Liquidated damages rationale

You agree actual damages are difficult to estimate (lost placement fees, opportunity costs, partner relationship impacts). Fees above are a reasonable estimate, not a penalty, and may be reduced only if a court requires.

### 9.5 Injunctive relief

Breach may cause irreparable harm. TradeQuip may seek injunctive relief in addition to monetary remedies to the extent permitted by law.

# RENDER MODEL (WHAT THE USER SEES ON SIGNUP)

**Render order:**

1. **Global Master (Document 1 Global)**
2. **Country/Region Addendum**
3. Optional: localized **Privacy Notice** link + **Risk Disclosures** link (separate docs, not required inside the contract text)

**Conflict rule:** Addendum overrides Global Master only for that jurisdiction.

**Acceptance record should store:**

* globalDocVersion, addendumId, addendumVersion
* countrySelected, regionDerived
* timestamp, ip/session/device fingerprint (as permitted)
* hash of rendered HTML/PDF text (tamper evidence)

# ALGORITHM (PSEUDOCODE) — SELECT + RENDER + LOG ACCEPTANCE

```text
INPUTS:
  userCountry (ISO2 from signup dropdown)
  userRegion (derived from ISO2 if needed)
  docs:
    globalMasterDoc("DOC1_GLOBAL", version)
    addendumDocs[addendumId](version)
    addendumMap (JSON above)

FUNCTION pickAddendum(userCountry, userRegion):
  if userCountry in addendumMap.countryAddendum:
      return addendumMap.countryAddendum[userCountry]
  else if userRegion in addendumMap.regionFallback:
      return addendumMap.regionFallback[userRegion]
  else:
      return addendumMap.defaultAddendum

FUNCTION renderAgreement(userCountry, userRegion):
  addendumId = pickAddendum(userCountry, userRegion)
  globalText  = load("DOC1_GLOBAL", "1.0")
  addendumText = load(addendumId, "1.0")

  rendered = concatenate(
      globalText,
      "\n\n---\n\n",
      "COUNTRY/REGION ADDENDUM (Auto-selected): " + addendumId,
      "\n\n",
      addendumText
  )

  return { rendered, addendumId, globalVersion:"1.0", addendumVersion:"1.0" }

ON SIGNUP SCREEN:
  when user selects country:
     addendumId = pickAddendum(country, derivedRegion(country))
     show label: "Terms loaded for: {country} / {addendumId}"
     render preview (scroll box)
  on click "I Accept":
     payload = renderAgreement(country, derivedRegion(country))
     hash = sha256(payload.rendered)
     store acceptance:
        userId, country, region, globalVersion, addendumId, addendumVersion,
        hash, acceptedAt, ip, userAgent, sessionId
     proceed to create account
```

## IMPLEMENTATION ADVICE: OPTION A VS OPTION B (PRACTICAL)

* **Option A (Global + Addendum)** is usually best for maintainability: one master contract + localized deltas.
* **Option B (full doc per country)** is simpler to render (one doc) but becomes operationally heavy (dozens of near-duplicate documents, higher legal update risk).

Given your scale, **Option A is the better long-term system**.
